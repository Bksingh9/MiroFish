**Goal:** Provide concrete proof. Lower the perceived risk of taking a meeting.

## Why this works

- **Reply-to-thread:** keeps the convo tight, signals continuity not nagging
- **Numbers, not adjectives:** "5+ days," "3 of 4," "~6 hours to ~45 minutes," "$22M"
- **Anonymous case study:** "6-person M&A boutique" is specific enough to be credible without naming the client (which they wouldn't approve in 4 days anyway). Once you have a named, approved case study, swap it in.
- **Same CTA:** consistent ask reduces decision friction

---

## If they replied to Touch 1 with curiosity

Skip Touch 2. Send a calendar link or the 90-second demo (build a Loom-style demo before you start outreach — see `agency/skills/morning_briefing/README.md` for talk track inspiration when we build it).

## If they replied to Touch 1 with "not interested"

Move them to `closed-lost` immediately. Don't argue. Send one line: *"Totally fair — I'll close the loop. If anything changes, you have my email."* That polite exit is what gets you back in the door 6 months later when their pain spikes.
