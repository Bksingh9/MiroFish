# Touch 3 — M&A Advisors / Business Brokers / PE Associates

**When to send:** Day 10 of the sequence (6 business days after Touch 2, still no reply).
**Goal:** Open-loop close. Make it psychologically easy to reply with "no" — which often produces a "yes" or a "later."
**Word count target:** Under 60 words.

---

## Subject line — keep the thread

**Reply to the same thread again.** Three messages in one thread is the maximum before it starts to feel pestering — Touch 3 is the last automated touch.

---

## Body

```
Last note — promise.

Two ways to close this:

1. "Send me the 90-second demo" — I'll fire it over, no call needed.
2. "Not relevant right now" — I'll close the loop and stop reaching out.

Either reply works. Silence I'll read as #2 and you won't hear from me again.

— {{OperatorFirstName}}
```

---

## Why this works

- **"Last note — promise"** signals you respect their time. Most cold outreach never closes the loop, which is why people stop replying.
- **Two explicit options + permission to be silent:** removes the awkwardness of replying. Most "nos" who get this email actually pick option 1, because it costs them nothing.
- **Forced binary:** "silence = no" makes the ghost an explicit choice they're agreeing to. Many reply just to *not* be that person.
- **Self-deprecating exit:** "you won't hear from me again" — pre-empts the "are you going to keep emailing me?" anxiety.

---

## What happens after Touch 3

| Outcome | Status update |
|---|---|
| Replied "send the demo" | Move to `proposal-sent` after sending. Schedule follow-up in 5 days. |
| Replied "not relevant" | Move to `dead`. Add a note. Re-engage in 6 months only if their situation changes. |
| Silence after 5 business days | Move to `dead`. Don't manually follow up — respect the implicit no. |

---

## After-send checklist

In `Pipeline` tab:
- Set `Last Touch Date` → today
- Increment `Touch Count` to 3
- Set `Follow-up Date` → today + 5 days (final wait window before marking `dead`)

---

## Re-engagement — 6 months later

The `dead` bucket isn't dead forever. Quarterly, run a re-engagement on anyone marked `dead` ≥ 6 months ago. New angle, new subject line, new value (e.g. a relevant case study, a new feature). Don't reuse this 3-touch sequence on them — you'll look like a bot.
